Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with many hidden layers to learn hierarchical representations of data. Early layers learn simple features such as edges, while deeper layers combine them into complex concepts such as faces or objects.

Deep learning became popular after 2012 because of three factors: large labelled datasets, powerful GPUs and improved training techniques. It achieves state-of-the-art results in image recognition, speech recognition and natural language processing, but it needs a lot of data and computation.
